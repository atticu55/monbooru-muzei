monbooru-muzei 1.9.2 (versionCode 12)

Changes vs 1.9.1:
- Settings page is now darker than the group cards in BOTH
  light and dark mode. (Previously day mode was inverted:
  the cards sat darker than the page, backwards from the
  Pixel Settings look. Dark mode already looked right.)
- Switches are now Material 3 fat-pill switches with a
  thumb icon: a check mark when on, an X when off.
  (Were the old thin AppCompat switches.)
- A thin gap line now separates the rows inside each
  group card, like the Pixel settings screenshot.

Theme: follows your system's day/night setting — including
sunrise/sunset schedules. No in-app override.
